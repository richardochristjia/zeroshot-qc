## Appendix — pinned decisions (Routers, decision-only unit)

- Fake/real selector: module-level factory indirection per router (e.g. `_get_segmenter()` / `_get_judge()` calling `build_segmenter(False)` / `build_judge(False)` sharing one `DiskCache`), mirroring `_get_sop_judge` (`backend/app/main.py:19`). Also recorded in `routers/inspect.py` docstring.

Also recorded in `routers/synth.py` docstring.
